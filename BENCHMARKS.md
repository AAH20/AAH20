# Domain benchmark protocols

These are measurement specifications for the seven independent engineering domains in the [profile](README.md). The profile cards show live **GitHub repository counts**, not benchmark scores. No customer outcome, production certification, or cross-project ranking is implied by a card.

Publish a numeric benchmark only with a pinned code revision, workload and denominator, input provenance, environment specification, baseline, raw results, calculation script, and the known failure cases. Mark synthetic, mocked, lab, and authorized production runs separately. Do not combine them into a single score.

| Domain | Primary metric | Exact calculation | Required workload and evidence | Interpretation limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commerce, revenue, and customer operations | Incident precision (%) | `100 × reviewer-confirmed actionable incidents / all surfaced incidents reviewed` | Freeze merchant/channel scope, SKU mapping, snapshot completeness, reviewer rubric, and adjudications. Report counts by incident kind. | A detected or resolved-observed mismatch does not prove incremental revenue. |
| Commerce, revenue, and customer operations | Reviewer minutes / 100 SKUs | `100 × recorded reviewer minutes / mapped SKUs assessed` | Time-stamped human workflow, same product set and review standard for baseline and tool-assisted runs. | Excludes untracked correction time unless explicitly added. |
| Cloud, platform engineering, and reliability | Unsafe-change escape rate (%) | `100 × unsafe proposals incorrectly allowed / all unsafe proposals in the frozen suite` | Fixed change set with expected policy decisions, blast-radius model, environment inventory, and independent labels. | A zero on a finite suite is not proof of safety for unseen infrastructure. |
| Cloud, platform engineering, and reliability | Verified rollback time (min) | `verification-complete timestamp − rollback trigger timestamp`, reported as median and p95 | Controlled environment, failure injection, operator record, rollback artifact, and post-rollback checks. | Lab rollback is not a production recovery-time objective. |
| GPU, model serving, and AI economics | p95 first-token latency (ms) | 95th percentile of request-to-first-output-token durations among the declared request population | Model revision, precision, GPU type/count, serving engine, concurrency, prompt/output lengths, warmup, and raw traces. | Uncomparable across different models or quality constraints. |
| GPU, model serving, and AI economics | Cost / 1M accepted tokens (USD) | `1,000,000 × fully allocated serving cost / accepted output tokens` | Time window, GPU rental or depreciation, utilization, power/network assumptions, rejection rubric, and token accounting. | Raw tokens/sec or GPU price alone cannot determine accepted-work cost. |
| Marketing, audiences, and growth | Incremental contribution / $ spent | `(treatment contribution − counterfactual contribution) / incremental media spend` | Randomized or defensible quasi-experimental design, fixed attribution window, contribution-margin inputs, exclusions, and uncertainty interval. | Correlation and synthetic audience reaction cannot establish lift. |
| Marketing, audiences, and growth | Experiment confidence-interval width (pp) | `upper bound − lower bound` for the predeclared lift estimate, in percentage points | Report estimator, confidence level, sample counts, missingness, and randomization or identification assumptions. | A narrow interval does not remove systematic bias. |
| Physical AI, robotics, and biometrics | Missed unsafe action rate (%) | `100 × unsafe actions not flagged before the declared decision deadline / unsafe actions in the labeled trial set` | Robot/task revision, sensor stack, safety envelope, trial video/telemetry, hazard labels, timing, and independent review. | Simulation and bench results do not certify real-world deployment. |
| Physical AI, robotics, and biometrics | Evidence loss rate (%) | `100 × required events absent or unverifiable / required events expected under the recorder contract` | Event schema, clock sync, fault-injection plan, storage recovery, replay verification, and custody boundary. | This is not aircraft recorder crash-survivability certification. |
| AI agents: runtime, security, identity, and governance | Unauthorized action pass rate (%) | `100 × prohibited tool actions executed / prohibited tool actions attempted in a frozen suite` | Identity, policy, tool schema, exact prompts and traces, independent expected decisions, and replayable tests. | Suite coverage does not establish universal resistance to attacks. |
| AI agents: runtime, security, identity, and governance | False-denial rate (%) | `100 × authorized actions denied / authorized actions attempted in the same frozen suite` | Same identities, policies, workloads, and adjudication process as the pass-rate test. | A lower false-denial rate must not be bought by permitting prohibited actions. |
| Data, simulation, and decision systems | Data freshness lag p95 (s) | 95th percentile of `query-visible timestamp − source-event timestamp` | Source clocks, ingestion windows, query samples, late events, replay logs, and error budget. | Fresh data may still be wrong or biased. |
| Data, simulation, and decision systems | Decision regret vs baseline (%) | `100 × (baseline utility − candidate utility) / abs(baseline utility)` on matched decisions | Frozen candidate set, utility function, baseline, outcome observation, censoring rules, and confidence interval. | If baseline utility is zero, report absolute difference instead; simulation is not realized outcome. |

Each repository owns its benchmark implementation and release evidence. This document standardizes the vocabulary for discovery and comparison; it does not assert that every repository already produces these metrics. When a repository reports a score, compare it only against runs with the same protocol version, workload, environment, and evidence class.
